LYNK 30 — Stop · Preserve · Report · Contain · Learn
LYNK 30 Protocol
1
🛑 STOP
Stop the affected activity when safe and practical. Do not continue actions that may worsen the incident.
2
🔒 PRESERVE
Preserve factual evidence. Do not alter, delete, or modify any records related to the incident.
3
📢 REPORT
Notify the designated manager or incident owner promptly. Do not make unauthorized public statements.
4
🔐 CONTAIN
Follow containment and communication instructions. Limit exposure and prevent further damage.
5
📚 LEARN
Record corrective and preventive actions. Conduct post-incident review within 72 hours of resolution.

LYNK 32 — 7-Step Protocol
1
🔒 Private Conversation
Never correct publicly. Immediate private 1:1 only.
2
📊 Evidence-Based
Show the data, not the emotion. Facts only.
3
🔍 Understand Why
Was it ignorance, pressure, or character? Determine root cause.
4
🎯 Connect to Goals
Link consequences to their personal goals and commission.
5
🤝 Co-Create Plan
They must own the correction plan. Co-design it together.
6
📝 Document Everything
Record per LYNK 32 and LYNK 27. Evidence preserved.
7
⏱️ 48hr Follow-Up
Fast feedback loop. Check in within 48 hours.
